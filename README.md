# ☕ Coffee Sales Performance Analysis
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing coffee sales performance from 2019-2022. Explores revenue trends, top customers, product performance, and geographic sales distribution to uncover growth opportunities and support data-driven business decisions using Excel, Pivot Tables, and dashboard design.

## Overview

This project analyzes coffee sales data to evaluate business performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product demand across multiple markets. Using Excel, Pivot Tables, Power Query, and interactive dashboards, the analysis transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights that support sales growth and customer retention strategies.

---

## Business Problem

A coffee retailer wants to better understand:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which countries contribute the highest sales?
- How are sales trending over time?
- What opportunities exist to increase revenue and customer retention?

This project provides a data-driven approach to answering these business questions and supporting strategic decision-making.

---

# Executive Summary

### Key Findings

✅ Arabica and Excelsa products generated the highest sales revenue.

✅ The United States contributed the largest share of total sales.

✅ A small group of customers generated a disproportionate percentage of overall revenue.

✅ Sales experienced clear seasonal fluctuations throughout the analysis period.

✅ Loyalty card holders consistently contributed higher sales volumes compared to non-members.

✅ Certain product sizes and roast types significantly outperformed others in customer demand.

# Analysis Approach

## 1. Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed monthly sales performance to identify growth patterns, seasonality, and revenue fluctuations.

### Key Insight

Sales performance showed recurring seasonal patterns, indicating opportunities for targeted promotional campaigns during slower periods.

---

## 2. Customer Analysis

Evaluated purchasing behavior across the customer base.

### Key Insight

Revenue was concentrated among a relatively small number of high-value customers, highlighting the importance of customer retention initiatives.

---

## 3. Product Performance Analysis

Compared revenue generated by different coffee products, roast types, and package sizes.

### Key Insight

Not all products contributed equally to revenue growth. Several products consistently outperformed others and should receive greater inventory and marketing focus.

---

## 4. Geographic Performance Analysis

Measured sales performance across countries and regions.

### Key Insight

The United States generated the highest sales volume, while international markets displayed varying levels of customer demand.

---

## 5. Loyalty Program Analysis

Examined the relationship between loyalty membership and purchasing behavior.

### Key Insight

Loyalty card holders demonstrated higher purchase frequency and greater revenue contribution than non-members.

---

# Insights Deep Dive

## Revenue Growth Is Driven By High-Performing Products

Certain coffee varieties consistently generated stronger sales performance throughout the reporting period.

### Business Impact

Understanding product demand allows management to:

- Optimize inventory planning
- Improve product marketing
- Prioritize high-margin products

### Recommendation

Increase promotional efforts around top-performing products and evaluate opportunities to expand similar offerings.

---

## Customer Revenue Is Highly Concentrated

A small group of customers account for a significant share of total sales.

### Business Impact

Retaining these customers is critical to maintaining revenue stability.

### Recommendation

Develop targeted retention campaigns for high-value customers through personalized promotions and loyalty rewards.

---

## Loyalty Program Drives Higher Spending

Customers enrolled in loyalty programs generate greater long-term value.

### Business Impact

Higher retention and repeat purchases directly contribute to revenue growth.

### Recommendation

Increase loyalty program adoption through onboarding incentives and exclusive member benefits.

---

## Geographic Opportunities Exist For Expansion

Sales distribution varies significantly across countries.

### Business Impact

Some markets remain underutilized and present growth opportunities.

### Recommendation

Deploy localized marketing strategies in lower-performing regions while maintaining investment in high-performing markets.

---

## Seasonal Demand Patterns Influence Sales

Sales fluctuate across different periods of the year.

### Business Impact

Understanding seasonality helps improve forecasting accuracy and resource allocation.

### Recommendation

Align promotional campaigns, staffing, and inventory planning with historical sales cycles.
